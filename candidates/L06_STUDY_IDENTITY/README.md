## Why archived

The study-versus-report distinction is real and CochraneForest supplies excellent external study↔paper gold. The problem is contribution size, not data quality.

Under the successful-result test, the strongest expected result is still readily compressed to an already-established evidence-synthesis principle: multiple reports of one study must not be counted as independent evidence. If flat input succeeds, the remaining scientific question becomes implicit publication linkage/entity resolution; if oracle grouping wins, the result largely confirms why systematic reviews already preserve study identity. Controlled wrong-split/wrong-merge experiments would make the evidence cleaner but do not by themselves create a new Main-level inference.

Reviewer compression remains:

> **“CochraneForest with a study-grouping ablation / publication-linkage stress test.”**

That is too strong to justify execution.
