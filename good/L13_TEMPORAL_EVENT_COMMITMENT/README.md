## Why archived

The original selected question asked whether temporal-relation processing prematurely commits an unresolved event as realized. The decisive pilot rejected that central behavioral bet under direct probing: models generally represented the non-veridicality of `before` correctly.

The strongest unexpected result then became a different paper identity: when asked to emit a plain timeline/structured representation, some models instantiated unresolved events as realized even though they answered the direct commitment question correctly. That is a generation/representation-construction phenomenon, not the original temporal-comprehension claim.

Under the corrected workflow, this mutation requires a fresh topic-selection and ownership audit before expansion. The project is archived rather than allowing the unexpected C5 result to inherit the original novelty approval and grow through additional experiments by momentum.
