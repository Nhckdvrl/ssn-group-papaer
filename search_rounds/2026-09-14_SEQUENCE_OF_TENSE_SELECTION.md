# 2026-09-14 — Sequence-of-Tense Selection Decision

## Decision

Register **L39 — Past Is Not Always Before / Sequence of Tense and Contextual Temporal Re-Anchoring** as:

> **`PILOT-AUTHORIZED — E01 ONLY; NOT MAINLINE`**

No claim is made that L39 is already a Main-level survivor. The authorization is for one cheap, bounded mother-phenomenon test.

---

## Why this candidate was selected

The user-provided style anchors are:

- *The Imperfective Paradox in Large Language Models*;
- *The Flexibility Trap: Rethinking the Value of Arbitrary Order in Diffusion Language Models*.

The desired research shape is not `complex mechanism work`. It is:

> **a durable, simple scientific distinction or widely held model belief -> a clean mismatch/pressure -> a small decisive experiment -> a broader conclusion about how modern models reason or compute.**

SOT fits that taste unusually well.

English past-under-past attitude reports create a clean form–meaning dissociation:

> `John said that Mary was sick.`

The lower past can support a reading in which Mary's sickness overlaps John's saying time. Therefore surface `PAST` does not force `BEFORE(matrix attitude time)`.

This permits a broader model-science question:

> **When a surface temporal cue conflicts with the context-relative interpretation licensed by compositional semantics, does the model re-anchor the cue or default to the surface heuristic?**

---

## Why this is not just `test another linguistic phenomenon`

A competence-only paper would ask whether models know SOT.

L39 instead seeks a three-part dissociation:

1. ordinary tense/temporal competence is intact;
2. the model may even be able to state the SOT rule explicitly;
3. contextual inference nevertheless overcommits to anteriority when embedded past must be interpreted relative to the matrix attitude time.

The potential contribution is therefore about **knowledge deployment / contextual temporal reference frames**, with SOT as the diagnostic instrument.

If this specificity does not appear, L39 dies.

---

## Data/gold advantage

Mucha, Renans & Romoli (2023) provide an unusually strong traditional substrate:

- English and Polish human experiment;
- 24 target lexicalizations in Experiment 1;
- 36 fillers;
- source-context tense × indirect-report tense manipulation;
- native English human ratings;
- full Appendix-A target materials;
- article under CC BY 4.0.

The E01 core therefore does **not** require a new dataset.

The strongest critical condition already exists in the traditional experiment:

> present-tense direct speech establishing simultaneity -> past-under-past indirect report.

The main methodological correction is to avoid assigning an ambiguous bare SOT sentence a single temporal label. Test licensing, compatibility and non-entailment instead.

No direct modern LLM owner was found for the parent quantity:

> **Does an LLM correctly deploy the licensed context-relative interpretation of past-under-past in consequence-sensitive inference, rather than treating past morphology as absolute anteriority?**

Nearby work on generic temporal ordering, tense probing/representation, and classic SOT semantics does not by itself own that quantity.

This novelty state is provisional. E01 requires one bounded owner sanity check before model execution.

## Strongest compression

> `This is another formal-semantics minimal-pair paper: does an LLM know SOT?`

This compression wins unless E01 shows a specific dissociation from ordinary temporal/tense competence and, ideally, from declarative SOT knowledge.

---

## E01 gate

Proceed only with the human-validated English core and matched controls.

Return to Selection only if **at least two strong current model families** show a stable SOT-specific failure / false-anteriority bias while explicit-anteriority and ordinary temporal controls are strong.

The strongest outcome is a knowledge–deployment gap.

Otherwise close L39 without multilingual, model, prompt, construction or mechanism rescue.

---

## Development boundary

If E01 survives:

- E02 may test small theory-controlled lexical robustness;
- E03 may test explicit SOT-rule reminders and selective recovery vs heuristic overcorrection;
- only then may an independent traditional past-under-past environment be considered.

None of these later stages is authorized by this selection decision.
