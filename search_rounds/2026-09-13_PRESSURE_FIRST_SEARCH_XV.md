## P74 — Did modern post-training abolish neural text degeneration under greedy/beam decoding?

**Status:** `DROP / CHANGED-REGIME RETEST ALREADY OWNED`

Holtzman et al. established the classic decoding law that likelihood-maximizing decoding can degenerate into repetition. A modern-regime retest initially looked attractive because low-temperature/deterministic decoding is now routine in strong instruction/reasoning models. However NeurIPS 2023 *Repetition In Repetition Out* already revisits the phenomenon across larger models and instruction tuning, identifies training-data repetition as a major cause, and explicitly tests Llama-2/base versus instruction-tuned variants. Instruction tuning can alleviate degeneration depending on the repetition statistics of the finetuning data. The old-law retest and the obvious changed-regime explanation are already owned.

**Anti-resurrection:** do not reopen `greedy decoding is fine now`, `instruction tuning fixed degeneration`, or `why modern models repeat less` with another model family.

## P77 — Why does SFT learn best from outputs the base model already finds probable?

**Status:** `DROP / DATA-CENTRIC OWNER PROGRAM + 2026 MECHANISTIC SUCCESSOR`

NeurIPS 2025 work on base-distribution-aligned response selection (GRAPE) reports that examples/responses with higher base-model probability can be more effective supervision. This initially suggested a model-science question about whether SFT mainly amplifies pre-existing policy support or learns new low-probability behavior. But the immediate literature is already moving from the selection law into token-level mechanism: 2026 ProFit explicitly connects base-token probability to semantic importance and preferentially trains high-probability/core logical tokens while suppressing replaceable low-probability expressions. More broadly the route is inseparable from a crowded data-selection/SFT-efficiency program that the project deliberately deprioritizes.

**Anti-resurrection:** do not reframe response filtering, base-probability matching, or probability-weighted SFT as a fresh mechanism paper unless a same-checkpoint causal quantity independent of data-selection utility emerges.

## P78 — Human-like constituent ordering: production accessibility vs parsing pressure in LMs

**Status:** `DROP / NON-IDENTIFYING MODEL-AS-HUMAN TRANSFER`

NAACL 2025 award work shows strong LMs reproduce human-like constituent-ordering preferences. Human theories separate production accessibility, dependency/locality and information-structure pressures. However an LM trained on human-produced text inherits the surface distribution generated by all of these causes. Reproducing the preference, or finding an internal correlate, cannot identify which cognitive cause generated the training regularity. A decisive causal answer would require counterfactual training distributions or a new independent instrument, changing the cost and paper identity.

**Anti-resurrection:** do not infer human production mechanism from LM ordering behavior or internal representation without an intervention that separates inherited distribution from intrinsic computation.
