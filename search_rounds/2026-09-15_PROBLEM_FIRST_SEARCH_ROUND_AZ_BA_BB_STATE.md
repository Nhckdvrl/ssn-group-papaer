**Question:** Why can downstream foundation models often be averaged, added/subtracted as task vectors, or merged directly in raw parameter coordinates even though neural-network parameters are symmetry-ridden and independently trained solutions have no canonical coordinate semantics?

## 3. WALL-BA — ellipsis / silent structure

**Question:** When language users interpret ellipsis, do they construct unpronounced syntactic structure at the ellipsis site, or can meaning be recovered through semantic/discourse anaphora or antecedent reactivation without generating silent syntax?

### Why this was a valid WALL

- decades-long linguistic ancestry;
- genuinely different structural, non-structural, discourse and processing theories;
- classic disagreement on voice mismatch, identity, connectivity, islands, processing cost and reconstruction/reactivation;
- the question is important without LLMs.

### Critical update

The strongest possible modern-LM story initially looked like `foundation-model internals make silent structure identifiable`.

That inference fails twice.

First, internal similarity/causal reuse cannot by itself distinguish silent syntactic reconstruction from semantic convergence, antecedent reactivation, or a generic predictive state.

Second, and more decisively, **Bruening, Koppy, Palaz, Tomioka & Tollan (2026), _How to understand silence: Voice mismatches in ellipsis in English_** explicitly set up **structure generation vs antecedent reactivation** and devise a human psycholinguistic identification using voice mismatch with an identical antecedent. Their critical contrast is not explained by reactivation alone and is reported as more consistent with structure generation.

Thus the most natural residual is not waiting for LLM access to make it identifiable.

**State:** `EXHAUSTED FOR CURRENT SEARCH / DIRECT HUMAN IDENTIFICATION EXISTS`.

Canonical file:
`search_rounds/2026-09-15_WALL_BA_ELLIPSIS_SILENT_STRUCTURE_EXHAUSTION.md`

## 4. WALL-BB — explicit linguistic units / tokenization

**Question:** What does explicit text segmentation contribute to language learning? Is a word/subword inventory a necessary linguistic/statistical inductive bias, or mainly a computational shortcut that a capable learner can reconstruct from raw character/byte sequences?

### Why this was the strongest-looking wall of the round

It passes nearly every early generator gate:

- very old scientific ancestry in segmentation, morphology and lexicon induction;
- a genuine foundation-model regime change: competitive byte/token-free models now exist;
- a clear tension: token-free learners can recover supra-character knowledge, yet explicit/tokenized boundaries still improve learning/efficiency;
- mature rival explanations on a shared quantity:
  - compression / sequence length / throughput;
  - vocabulary scaling;
  - linguistic/statistical boundary prior.

The lineage is continuous:

- TACL 2019 `Tabula Nearly Rasa`: word-like boundary tracking and linguistic knowledge emerge from unsegmented character input;
- CANINE / ByT5: explicit fixed token vocabularies are not architecturally necessary;
- EMNLP 2024 `Tokenization Is More Than Compression`: minimizing token count does not explain downstream quality;
- ACL 2025 BLT: byte-level scaling with dynamic patches;
- 2025–2026 morphology-aware tokenization: boundary alignment can matter beyond raw token count.

### Decisive owner collision after the residual was independently derived

**Gigant, Peng & Quesnelle (2026), _Decoupling the Benefits of Subword Tokenization for Language Model Training via Byte-level Simulation_** directly isolates, inside a controlled byte-level pipeline:

- training/sample throughput;
- vocabulary scaling;
- subword-boundary linguistic priors.

The paper concludes that throughput and explicit/inductive boundary information are central sources of the subword advantage.

This is almost exactly the residual uncertainty that emerged from our own lineage map.

**State:** `EXHAUSTED / CONTINUOUS DIRECT PROGRAM EXISTS`.

`search_rounds/2026-09-15_WALL_BB_TOKENIZATION_UNIT_INDUCTIVE_BIAS_EXHAUSTION.md`
