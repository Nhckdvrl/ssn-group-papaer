# 3. WALL-G — IP05 / systematic generalization

Audit:
- `search_rounds/2026-09-15_WALL_G_SYSTEMATICITY_EXHAUSTION.md`

Commit:
- `9d393f8f59778036c2b54cc2b395a3fe71be470d`

## Historical correction

The Fodor–Pylyshyn challenge is not simply `can the network get an OOD split right?`.

The deeper object is:

> why are structurally related cognitive capacities grouped rather than punctate?

Modern held-out accuracy is evidence about this object, not the object itself.

## Live dispute

- Lake & Baroni 2023: MLC achieves human-like systematic compositional behavior.
- Goodale & Mascarenhas TACL 2026: the original challenge still stands; MLC shows substantial within- and out-of-distribution unsystematicity.

This is a genuine 1988→2026 intellectual dispute.

## Direct modern owners

The obvious missing explanatory pieces are already active programs:

- what meta-learning installs: ACL 2025 Goodale et al.;
- learned variable binding: ICML 2025 Wu / Geiger / Millière;
- causal compositional circuit: Tang / Lake / Jazayeri 2026;
- data entropy / coverage accounts;
- task symmetry / representation-geometry accounts;
- iterated-learning emergence of compositionality/systematicity.

## Strongest attempted residual

Return to the original quantity by estimating the **joint distribution over structurally related capacities across independent learners**, rather than average OOD accuracy.

Killed because:

- Goodale 2026 already demonstrates punctate / unsystematic behavior;
- large-population / multi-seed analyses already exist;
- covariance is descriptive, not an explanation for why capacities group;
- turning it into an index becomes evaluator construction.

## Second attempted residual: causal systematicity

Davies-style causal common factors appeared to offer a perfect `old problem + new intervention` route.

Killed because:

- Budding 2025 directly imports causal systematicity into LLMs and proposes intervention-based evidence;
- variable-binding / compositional-circuit papers already provide direct experimental neighbors;
- a new paper compresses to `classic systematicity × mechanistic interpretability`.

## Decision

**Current IP05 descendants exhausted.**

Keep IP05 ACTIVE as an important unsolved scientific problem. Reopen only when two mature accounts make opposite predictions about the same pre-declared capacity family under one identifying treatment.

# 4. WALL-H — IP11 / reachable behavioral support

Audit:
- `search_rounds/2026-09-15_WALL_H_REACHABLE_BEHAVIORAL_SUPPORT_EXHAUSTION.md`

Commit:
- `b2fc920f27e53e0d5bf7058b38d445d99238bb27`

## Mother problem

Observed output is not identical to latent capability.

Deep ancestors include:

- Tulving & Pearlstone 1966 availability vs accessibility;
- competence vs performance;
- generative-model mode coverage;
- exploration / behavioral repertoire;
- probabilistic typicality rather than modal decoding.

## Critical conceptual finding

Literal mathematical support is almost useless for ordinary softmax LMs: almost every finite token string has nonzero probability.

Any scientifically useful `support` must therefore be **effective / accessible support** under a specified resource or intervention class.

But different choices create different scientific objects:

- finite-k iid sampling;
- decoding policy;
- prompt search;
- activation steering;
- fine-tuning;
- adaptation-program length;
- semantic mode coverage.

There is no universally privileged accessible set.

## Direct frontier owners

- NeurIPS 2025 RLVR capacity-boundary paper: large-k pass@k; reweighting vs genuinely new reasoning patterns.
- NeurIPS 2024 / ICML 2025 capability elicitation: hidden capabilities under prompts / fine-tuning.
- ICML 2026 task complexity: shortest adaptation program as a resource-sensitive accessibility quantity.
- 2023 RLHF generalization/diversity work and NAACL 2025 conceptual-diversity work.
- ICLR 2026 policy contraction: Support Retention Ratio.
- ICML 2026 Flexibility Trap: decoding policy can collapse effective solution diversity.

## Strongest attempted residual

> When alignment removes a behavior from ordinary generations, was it erased or merely made inaccessible?

Scientifically natural, but killed because it reviewer-compresses to:

> alignment diversity-collapse + capability elicitation / unlearning-style recovery.

The deletion-vs-suppression distinction is itself mature and highly active.

## Decision

**WALL-H exhausted as a topic generator.**

Keep IP11 as a warning that top-1 / average performance does not define capability. Do not invent another pass@k / support / semantic-diversity metric without a substantive theory that fixes the access resource.
