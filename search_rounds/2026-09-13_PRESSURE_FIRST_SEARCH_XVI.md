## P79 — Where does constructional meaning come from: an abstract construction or lexical composition?

**Status:** `PRESSURE ONLY — IDENTIFICATION / MOTHER BLOCKER — NO COMPUTE`

### Pressure
Construction Grammar gives a simple scientific object: in `She sneezed the napkin off the table`, the verb `sneeze` does not itself lexicalize caused motion, yet the sentence receives a caused-motion interpretation. The computational question is whether an LM contributes event structure from an abstract caused-motion construction or merely composes lexical/contextual associations online.

### Why not promoted
The modern behavioral mother is not yet strong enough: 2025 work on caused-motion meaning finds that many strong LMs still fail to reliably infer the construction-contributed motion component. ACL 2026 Outstanding CxMP likewise finds that constructional semantic understanding develops later and remains substantially weaker than shallow form/grammaticality cues.

The obvious mechanistic experiment—extract/patch a construction representation into a novel verb frame—is also non-identifying. ACL Findings 2026 already owns compact causal semantic-role/event-role circuitry, so `CxMP/caused-motion behavior + semantic-role circuit + standard patching` compresses the strongest naive paper. A transferred caused-motion preference would not by itself establish a construction-level source rather than lexical/event-role feature injection.

### Blocker
Need a selective operation or matched contrast that distinguishes construction-supplied event structure from lexical/event-role composition without injecting the target meaning. Until then this remains a real theoretical pressure but not a compute candidate.

## P81 — Does instruction tuning separate propositional content from directive force?

**Status:** `DROP / DIRECT PRAGMATIC-REPRESENTATION OWNER + SAFETY ADJACENCY`

A linguistically appealing question is whether an LM represents what an utterance says separately from whether it functions as a request/command, e.g. direct imperative versus quoted/reported/conditional directive. But LREC 2026 already uses speech-act theory to compare base and instructed LMs and reports that instruction tuning reorganizes middle-layer representations from syntactic form toward pragmatic intent, including convergence among different surface forms of requests. A causal extension would also sit immediately next to prompt-injection/quoted-instruction work, an already dense and deprioritized safety/system lane.

**Anti-resurrection:** a new quoted-command dataset or activation patch does not create independent ownership.
