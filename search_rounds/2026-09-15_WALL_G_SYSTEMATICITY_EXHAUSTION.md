# 1. The mother problem is not `OOD accuracy`

The classic Fodor–Pylyshyn challenge was not simply:

> can a neural network answer a novel combination correctly?

The deeper object is a **law over capacities**:

> why does possessing one cognitive capacity imply possession of a family of structurally related capacities?

The familiar example is that a system capable of understanding `John loves Mary` should, barring independent lexical deficits, also be capable of understanding structurally related thoughts such as `Mary loves John`.

A theory of systematicity therefore owes more than successful interpolation. It should explain why related capacities are **non-punctate** — why they come as a package.

This distinction matters because much modern `compositional generalization` work has changed the scientific observable from:

> distribution / causal organization of related capacities

to:

> accuracy on a held-out train/test split.

The latter can be useful evidence, but it is not automatically the original explanatory target.

---

# 2. Historical theory branches

## 2.1 Classical constituent structure

Fodor & Pylyshyn argue that classical systems explain systematicity because the same constituents and combinatorial operations are reused across related thoughts. If `John`, `Mary`, and `loves(x,y)` are genuine reusable constituents, the family of related thoughts is generated by construction.

The challenge to connectionism was therefore architectural / explanatory:

> what in the system guarantees the relevant grouping of capacities?

## 2.2 Distributed connectionist replies

Smolensky, Chalmers and later connectionist work reject the inference that systematic computation requires literal classical symbolic architecture. Distributed representations can implement structure-sensitive operations and variable-like behavior.

But this moves rather than deletes the challenge: if a trained network can in principle instantiate a systematic computation, **why does learning produce the right shared organization rather than a punctate collection of local mappings?**

## 2.3 Strong systematicity and learnability

Hadley and successors sharpen the empirical requirement: familiar constituents should generalize to genuinely novel structural roles / combinations.

This spawned the modern train/test tradition, culminating in SCAN-like and related compositional-generalization evaluations.

The gain was operational clarity. The cost was that one split can test one transfer without explaining why the entire family of capacities is grouped.

## 2.4 Common-component / causal theories

Davies, Phillips & Wilson, and related work make the explanatory demand explicit: related input–output transformations should share a **common causal component** corresponding to their common semantic/structural pattern.

Phillips & Wilson's category-theoretic program treats systematicity as a distribution of cognitive capacities and asks for a construction from which the related capacities follow rather than being independently stipulated.

This lineage also highlights **quasi-systematicity**: humans are highly but not perfectly systematic, so a useful theory must explain both the grouping of capacities and principled boundaries / exceptions.

# 3. The 2023–2026 dispute is genuinely the old dispute, not a paper gap

## 3.1 Lake & Baroni 2023 — strong behavioral claim

`Human-like systematic generalization through a meta-learning neural network` argues that a standard neural network trained with Meta-Learning for Compositionality (MLC) can reproduce human-like systematic generalization while retaining flexibility.

This is important because the paper explicitly positions the result as answering a classic connectionist challenge rather than merely improving a benchmark.

But the model's strongest generalization is conditional on the meta-training distribution; the paper itself notes failures on sufficiently out-of-distribution rules / productivity cases.

## 3.2 Goodale & Mascarenhas 2026 — explicit rebuttal

TACL 2026, `Fodor and Pylyshyn's Systematicity Challenge Still Stands`, directly rejects the conclusion that MLC settles the classic challenge. The model struggles with even modest rule shifts outside its meta-training distribution and also behaves unsystematically on many within-distribution cases.

This is exactly the kind of lineage we want:

> 1988 theoretical demand → 2023 claimed neural resolution → 2026 direct reanalysis / failed inference.

However, a live dispute does not imply that an unowned experiment remains for us.

# 4. Modern owner map: the obvious missing pieces are already active programs

## 4.1 `What does meta-learning install?` is directly owned

Goodale, Mascarenhas & Lakretz, ACL 2025, `Meta-Learning Neural Mechanisms rather than Bayesian Priors`, explicitly test what meta-training imbues a model with.

Contrary to a simplicity-prior interpretation, they find evidence that meta-training installs neural mechanisms such as counters; a single mechanistically rich formal language can confer as much downstream benefit as thousands of meta-training languages.

Therefore:

> `MLC works because it learned mechanism X rather than prior Y`

is already a direct parent.

## 4.2 `Does a Transformer learn variable binding?` is directly owned

Wu, Geiger & Millière, ICML 2025, identify a developmental transition from heuristic behavior to systematic variable binding in symbolic programs. Causal interventions show the residual stream functioning as addressable memory with specialized attention routing.

This directly occupies the route:

> classic symbolic requirement → learned neural implementation → causal mechanism.

## 4.3 `What circuit supports compositional generalization?` is directly owned

Tang, Lake & Jazayeri 2026 reverse-engineer a Transformer circuit for compositional generalization, using causal ablations and steering. The model disentangles token position and identity and implements a general remapping algorithm.

Thus a new project of the form

> `take a successful systematic-generalization behavior and mechanistically find its reusable circuit`

is a behavior→mechanism sequel, not a new scientific parent.

## 4.4 Data-distribution explanations are active

Wold et al., Findings ACL 2025, connect systematic-generalization difficulty to entropy of component distributions and show that high-entropy regimes can support strong generalization without special built-in priors.

Nature Communications 2024 trains many neural learners across artificial languages of varying compositional structure and finds more compositional input yields more systematic, more mutually convergent and more human-like generalization.

Iterated-learning work in 2026 further studies how compositionality/systematicity can emerge culturally rather than solely from learner architecture.

So:

> `maybe systematicity comes from the training distribution, not architecture`

is an established research program, not an unasked question.

## 4.5 Task-structure / symmetry / geometry explanations are active

Recent theory has moved beyond architecture-vs-data slogans:

- Hwang & Park 2026 argue intrinsic task symmetry drives the memorization→generalization transition and representation geometry across algorithmic domains.
- Chang, Mucsányi & Oh 2026 derive structural constraints on feature geometry from systematic first-order update propagation, with tensor-factorized structure for negation/converse and an obstruction for composition.
- Pattern-matching theory in ICLR 2026 formalizes when compositional tasks are solvable from functional equivalence / coverage and when path ambiguity creates a structural barrier.

Therefore `what property of the task makes systematicity possible?` is also already a rapidly developing theory program.

# 5. The most promising return to the original quantity — and why it still dies

The most intellectually faithful modern descendant was:

> **Do independently trained neural learners actually exhibit the biconditional grouping of capacities that defines systematicity, or are successful models punctate — capable of arbitrary subsets of structurally related transformations?**

Instead of averaging test accuracy, one could train many independent learners and estimate the joint distribution over a pre-specified family of related capacities.

This is attractive because it returns to the old object:

> systematicity as a law over which capacity combinations are possible / probable.

### Why it does not survive Selection

1. **Goodale & Mascarenhas 2026 already establish the relevant failure mode.** Their central claim is precisely that MLC can behave unsystematically / punctately, including within the intended meta-training regime.
2. **Population / seed analyses are already natural in the modern literature.** Work with hundreds or thousands of neural learners already measures convergence, variability and systematic-generalization structure.
3. **A covariance table is descriptive, not explanatory.** Even a beautifully estimated distribution over capacities would not reveal why capacities group or fracture.
4. **Turning the distribution into a new scalar/index would be evaluator creation.** That is contrary to the current project taste and still would not solve the causal challenge.
5. **The successful-result upper bound is weak.** Finding that some seeds are punctate mostly strengthens the 2026 critique; finding strong covariance re-establishes a behavioral regularity but does not identify the shared mechanism.

### Verdict

**KILL — interesting correction to measurement, insufficient independent contribution.**

# 6. Causal systematicity looked like genuine modern leverage — but it is also occupied

A sharper descendant was to return to Davies-style **causal systematicity**:

> related transformations should be mediated by a causal common factor corresponding to their shared semantic / structural pattern.

Modern neural networks offer interventions that older philosophical work did not have. This initially looks like a perfect `old problem + new instrument` shape.

But two things kill the route.

## 6.1 Direct conceptual owner

Budding, Philosophy of Science 2025, explicitly imports Davies' causal-systematicity criterion into LLMs and proposes intervention behavior as a way to distinguish shared causal structure from one-to-one memorized mappings. The paper uses model-editing evidence as preliminary evidence for causal common factors.

Thus the conceptual move

> `use modern interventions to test Davies-style causal systematicity in LLMs`

is already direct prior art.

## 6.2 Direct experimental neighbors

Variable-binding and compositional-circuit studies already use causal intervention to identify shared reusable computations in trained Transformers.

A new NLP paper would therefore reviewer-compress to:

> `Budding's causal systematicity + Wu/Tang-style mechanistic tools on another linguistic family.`

That is exactly the prohibited `old linguistic/cognitive phenomenon × mechanistic interpretability` generator.

### Verdict

**KILL — direct conceptual owner + mechanism-successor compression.**

# 7. Other natural descendants closed

## G1 — Symbolic constituent mechanism vs distributed binding mechanism

Not a clean same-quantity horse race. Modern connectionist accounts can implement variable binding / reusable computation without literal classical tokens, and the right abstraction level is itself disputed.

A probe / causal-abstraction comparison would inherit the alignment / intervention-identification problems already recorded under WALL-A and the lexical-event-structure exhaustion audit.

**Verdict: NO CLEAN IDENTIFICATION.**

## G2 — Meta-learned Bayesian prior vs learned neural primitive

Directly owned by ACL 2025 Goodale et al.

**Verdict: DIRECT OWNER.**

## G3 — Architecture bias vs training-distribution entropy

These are not mutually exclusive mature accounts. Architecture and distribution jointly determine learning, and recent work directly studies both.

`hold one fixed and sweep the other` would be factorial knob shopping, not a new scientific law.

**Verdict: FALSE RIVALRY / A+B.**

## G4 — Symmetry vs coverage / pattern matching

Again not a clean rivalry. Symmetry describes structure of the target computation; coverage / entropy controls what evidence exposes that structure. Both can be simultaneously necessary.

A head-to-head benchmark would not identify a new causal variable.

**Verdict: NO SAME-QUANTITY OPPOSITION.**

## G5 — Scaling causes systematicity

Directly occupied by scaling / entropy / task-space-coverage studies. The result would be another scaling law / benchmark curve.

**Verdict: CROWDED.**

## G6 — Natural-language argument roles / morphology / filler-gap as the systematic family

This degenerates into `known linguistic phenomenon × systematicity test` or `behavior → mechanism` and collides with argument-structure, morphology, filler-gap and variable-binding literatures.

**Verdict: PROHIBITED GENERATOR SHAPE.**

## G7 — Quasi-systematicity: why are humans systematic only in some contexts?

This remains scientifically deep. The historical theory explicitly recognizes constrained exceptions.

But no current modern operation supplies an identifying contrast. Simply cataloguing where LMs generalize or fail creates another benchmark. A mechanism study requires a prior theory of why one context should share a causal component and another should not.

**Verdict: IMPORTANT RESIDUAL, NO CURRENT ATTACK.**

# 9. Why the live 2023–2026 dispute does not automatically give us a paper

The Lake–Baroni / Goodale–Mascarenhas disagreement is scientifically real, but the remaining uncertainty is too high-level:

> **What architectural / learning principles are sufficient to guarantee or strongly induce the human-like grouping of related capacities?**

This is essentially the original 1988 challenge.

The current frontier supplies many partial answers — meta-learned primitives, variable-binding mechanisms, entropy/coverage, symmetry, representation geometry, iterated learning — but they do not yet form two mature, mutually exclusive theories that make opposite predictions under one natural treatment.

Inventing our own scalar to compare them would be premature theory aggregation, not discovery.

Therefore the existence of a beautiful old unresolved problem is not enough to justify a new project now.

# 11. Reopen condition

Reopen this WALL only if one of the following appears:

1. a theory specifies a **pre-declared equivalence class of capacities** that should necessarily share one computation, not merely a train/test split;
2. two mature accounts make opposite predictions about the **same learner, same capacity family, same treatment, same observable**;
3. a modern intervention can test whether a **necessary causal common factor** is reused across the entire capacity family without relying on a post-hoc alignment / arbitrary representation map;
4. the result remains informative if the common factor is found, absent, heterogeneous, or only partially shared;
5. the strongest result cannot be compressed to `another compositional-generalization benchmark`, `behavior→mechanism sequel`, or `old systematicity challenge × LLM`.

Until then:

> **IP05 is an important unsolved problem without a currently justified project descendant.**
