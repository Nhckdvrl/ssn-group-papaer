## Calibration anchors

### ACL 2026 Best — The Imperfective Paradox in Large Language Models

Transferable paper shape:

- a durable, natural scientific distinction that is understandable from one example;
- independently defensible gold/minimal contrast;
- a specific wrong computation (teleological completion bias), not merely a benchmark error rate;
- controlled contrasts that distinguish the scientific explanation;
- representation / behavior evidence that deepens the same question;
- intervention results that expose a boundary/trade-off rather than mutate the paper identity.

Do **not** mechanically generate another classic linguistic phenomenon and gamble that strong LLMs fail.

### EMNLP 2026 Main — CAROT

Transferable paper shape:

- identify that an existing operation acts on the wrong scientific object or collapses two quantities that should be separated;
- decompose a representation into a transferable/shared component and a load-bearing specific component;
- derive the method from the scientific diagnosis rather than inventing a method first;
- validate the proposed target causally at inference time and then show that training can internalize it;
- preserve a meaningful quantity (language-specific information / output-language consistency) while improving the intended shared capability.

Do **not** clone CAROT into another modality/task where shared-vs-specific representation alignment is already a crowded parent.

## Killed during this search round

### Implicit-memory success-vs-failure adaptation mechanism

Lead: Why can LLMs learn to repeat successful behavior much more readily than learn to inhibit failed behavior?

Decision: **INTERNAL HISTORY COLLISION — DO NOT REOPEN.**

Reason: `good/L10_FROM_FAILURE_TO_ACTION/` already used ImplicitMemBench as a mother phenomenon and decomposed failure experience into outcome memory → attribution → replacement policy → action. On 2026-09-11 L10 was archived because even its strongest expected result compressed to a finer decomposition of already-established diagnosis/self-repair gaps. Changing the decomposition or benchmark does not create a new paper identity.

### Wrong-CoT / negative-rationale distillation: what information transfers?

Lead: If incorrect reasoning traces can still improve a student, is the transferred object logical structure, semantic features, or a few high-value tokens?

Decision: **EXTERNAL PARENT COLLISION — DO NOT PROMOTE.**

Reason: prior CoT-distillation work already studies post-hoc rationales, token permutation/masking, and token-level contribution/gradient explanations. The natural development path is therefore a narrower mechanism cell inside an occupied `why does imperfect rationale distillation work?` parent.

### Subliminal-learning hidden-signal mechanism

Lead: What internal signal allows behavior/traits to transfer through apparently unrelated generated data?

Decision: **EXTERNAL SUCCESSOR COLLISION — DO NOT PROMOTE.**

Reason: the original phenomenon has already accumulated mechanistic successors localizing transfer to a small subset of divergence tokens / early-layer signals and following training-time trait-direction dynamics. `find the hidden carrier` is no longer open enough for a new paper identity.

### Generic factual-knowledge vs shortcut arbitration

Lead: When a model possesses exact factual/numerical knowledge but sometimes chooses a shortcut, what controller decides which pathway wins?

Decision: **DO NOT PROMOTE IN GENERIC FORM.**

Reason: 2026 work now directly studies evidence arbitration, adaptive trust, confidence-causal behavior, and metacognitive control, while ACL/EACL work already identifies factual-vs-shortcut internal pathways. A generic `confidence gates whether the model uses knowledge` result is highly compressible and has an L12-style claim-mutation risk. Reconsider only if a distinct natural arbitration law with a new decisive prediction is found.
