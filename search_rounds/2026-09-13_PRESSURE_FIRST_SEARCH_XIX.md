## P95 — Linguistic convergence: partner-specific adaptation or context-wide entrainment?

**Status:** `DROP / DIRECT 2026 OWNER`

### Pressure
EACL 2026 *Do language models accommodate their users? A study of linguistic convergence* establishes a strong mother: LMs converge to users' language, often more strongly than humans, while the authors explicitly hypothesize that the underlying mechanism differs from human convergence. Classic dialogue theory provides a clean distinction between partner-specific conceptual pacts and partner-independent/context-wide alignment.

The attractive matched test is intuitive: keep exactly the same prior linguistic evidence but change whether it was produced by the current interlocutor or by another speaker, asking whether later convergence is indexed to partner identity or merely to recent context.

### Why dead
June 2026 *Aligned but Not Partner-Specific: Distinguishing How Multimodal LLM Agents Succeed in Reference Games Without Human-Like Conventions* already performs essentially this scientific discrimination using real versus pseudo dyads. It finds agent alignment without the human partner-specific convention signature. 2025 work on agent-to-agent Theory of Mind/interlocutor awareness further makes partner identity an explicit model variable. Moving from multimodal reference-game lexical alignment to pure-text style/syntax changes the phenotype, not the parent scientific question.

**Reviewer compression:** `EACL 2026 convergence mother + 2026 real-vs-pseudo dyad partner-specificity test = alignment need not be partner-indexed.`

**Anti-resurrection:** do not reopen `does the model adapt to this person or just the context?`, speaker-swap style convergence, conceptual-pact tests, or text-only partner-specific entrainment unless a qualitatively different scientific quantity appears.

## P96 — At-issue content: explicit discourse-priority state or surface continuation bias?

**Status:** `PRESSURE ONLY — IDENTIFICATION BLOCKER — NO COMPUTE`

### Pressure
EACL 2026 *Hey, wait a minute: on at-issue sensitivity in Language Models* establishes that LMs preferentially continue at-issue rather than backgrounded content, that instruction tuning strengthens this preference, and that discourse cues can modulate it. This supplies a credible behavioral mother for a classical discourse-status variable.

A mechanistic question could ask whether the model constructs a causal discourse-priority/QUD-like state that determines what information controls continuation, or whether the same behavior is produced by surface structural and local-likelihood cues without an independently represented discourse status.

### Why not promoted
The obvious next experiment is only `decode/patch at-issueness`, which is not identifying. Main-clause position, syntactic embedding, recency, lexical cueing and discourse status covary in natural constructions; a causal direction for at-issue examples could encode any combination of these. The mother paper already manipulates discourse cues behaviorally, but no operation has yet been found that changes discourse status while preserving the relevant proposition and surface support strongly enough to discriminate `discourse state` from `surface continuation statistics`.

**Blocker:** a theory-grounded same-content operation that changes the QUD/at-issue relation without directly injecting which proposition should be continued, plus construction-level negative controls. Until then, no candidate and no GPU.

## P97 — Irony: pragmatic intent computed but not integrated into the final decision?

**Status:** `DROP / DIRECT 2026 MECHANISTIC OWNER + REDUNDANT IDENTIFICATION SHAPE`

ACL 2026 Main *LLMs in Sarcasm Detection? It's elementary! (Or is it?)* already triangulates hidden-state geometry, entropy and causal interventions and attributes synthetic-data success to shortcut signatures rather than pragmatic irony cues. Findings ACL 2026 separately reports `intent-irony decoupling`, where models can recover subsidiary pragmatic signals yet fail the holistic irony decision. A new `intent is represented but final judgment ignores it` causal patch would therefore be both owner-adjacent and another instance of the portfolio-redundant state/readout skeleton.

**Anti-resurrection:** do not reopen sarcasm/irony as `pragmatic intent present but unread`, `context conflict shortcut`, or `surface cue vs ToM mechanism` with a new model or intervention.

## P98 — Modern chat models stopped falling for semantic illusions: changed semantic processing or late correction policy?

**Status:** `DROP / K174-LIKE POST-TRAINING CAPABILITY-VS-READOUT PARENT`

### Pressure
Nature Computational Science 2023 reported a striking regime transition: increasingly capable pre-chat LMs became strongly human-like on semantic illusions, while ChatGPT-3.5/4 largely stopped giving the intuitive illusion response and instead detected the invalid premise. This superficially invites a strong training-stage question: did post-training change the underlying semantic/comprehension computation or preserve the heuristic response tendency while adding a later correction policy?

### Why dead
The scientific distinction collapses to the already-killed generic post-training `capability/internal state vs readout/policy` parent (K174 family). The semantic-illusion phenotype does not itself provide a new selective operation. Base-vs-instruct comparison, hidden-state probes, activation patching or an answer-policy intervention would merely instantiate that generic distinction on a classic cognitive effect. Current work on premise accommodation/epistemic vigilance also explicitly studies when LMs challenge false premises rather than accommodate them.

**Anti-resurrection:** do not reopen `ChatGPT fixed the Moses illusion because semantics changed vs policy changed` unless an illusion-specific causal quantity appears that cannot be compressed into generic post-training elicitation/readout.

## P99 — Generics/quantifiers causally set inductive strength

**Status:** `DROP CURRENT FORM / BEHAVIOR ALREADY OWNS THE INFERENCE; PATCHING ADDS LITTLE`

Findings ACL 2026 *Bears, all bears, and some bears* shows VLMs reproduce the human ordering `all > generics > some` in novel-property induction and reports representational organization by inductive constraints rather than surface form. A tempting causal continuation would swap/patch the quantifier-state representation and test whether property induction changes.

But the strongest successful result would largely restate the already-observed behavioral fact that changing the linguistic quantifier changes induction. Showing a corresponding causal representation is useful mechanistic confirmation, not yet a new Main-level parent. No distinct theoretical A/B or selective operation has been identified whose answer is not already strongly implied by the mother.

**Anti-resurrection:** do not promote `generic/all/some feature is causal`, quantifier-state patching, or another novel-property induction probe without a new competing computational account.
