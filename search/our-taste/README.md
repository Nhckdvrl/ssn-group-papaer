# Our Taste Search

**Role:** select a **problem territory worth exploring** under our own research taste.

Scope is intentionally broad: NLP or non-NLP, hot or non-hot, analysis or method, training or training-free, systems, agents, robotics, multimodal, AI4Science/Quant, etc.

This is a search lane, not an experiment workspace and not a candidate list.

The structural contract is:

> find a high-value area with a real baseline / object / pain point → hand it to `../../workbench/` → reproduce and strengthen the baseline → explore → learn from successes and failures → allow the final paper identity to emerge later.

Do not put topic folders or experiments here.

Historical “chasing trends” search documents are preserved under:
`../../archive/search/chasing-trends/`.
