## P1 already changes the premise

1. JGP's weakest ZH benchmark scores do not preferentially benefit from pure adjacency. ID does. Two languages, dissimilar task sets, one seed and MT-selected checkpoints prevent a causal acquisition-state inference. Compare shared XCOPA/XStoryCloze separately, not language macro scores over different tasks.
2. OpenSeal's XNLI lift is small, mixed across languages, absent at 7B; XCOPA does not improve even at 1B. PAWS-X improves in both EN and ZH, including EN +2.65/+3.65. This motivates task/content explanations, not a generic target-language acquisition effect. Section 6.1 also has a **multilingual replacement** control: preserve replay, EN texts and adjacency format, replace SEA translations by unrelated SEA-PILE text. At 7B, authentic pairing raises MT +9.09/+14.85 BLEU and PAWS-X +2.25 pp, but XNLI -0.73 and XCOPA +0.37 are not significant. This is valuable existing evidence, although replacement changes SEA semantic content/quality; it is not a same-token shuffle control.
3. No parent directly factorially manipulates competence while holding pairing and content fixed. Cross-paper contrast identifies a question, not a causal transition.
4. Regressing change on noisy baseline accuracy induces mathematical coupling/regression-to-mean. Competence diagnostics must use independent held-out text or disjoint items; descriptive per-language effects are not evidence of a causal state variable.

## Two additional named substrates

- [False Friends](https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-emnlp.1153/): 24 autoregressive bilingual models (85M non-embedding parameters), XLM-R token segmentation held fixed; vary shared token IDs. CCMatrix controls semantic content. **Downstream scores involve English supervised fine-tuning**, then zero-shot transfer to L2. No-overlap hurts transfer heavily even though some embedding semantic similarity remains. This is not equivalent to frozen decoder few-shot reasoning; useful for separating latent capability from readout transfer.
- [Macaroni](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30535): ~98M GPT-2, equal EN/NL/ZH, 100M-word corpus, 30 epochs, 8 seeds; CS/non-CS × shuffled/curriculum plus controls. Alignment changes strongly; downstream leaderboard gain significant only CS/curriculum vs non-CS/curriculum (p=.016), **not** vs non-CS/shuffled (p=.41). Alignment persists after monolingual final stage, unlike JGP translation forgetting. Main gain is in fine-tuned tasks. Synthetic CS is coherent translation insertion; MONOWEB natural CS is a different intervention.
- [Bilingual BabyLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29552), read v2: EN/ES synthetic TinyDialogues, GPT-2 124M/39M and GPT-BERT, 100M/20M-word data, up to 3 seeds. Exposure ratios 90/10 through 10/90 are varied in the ordinary multilingual condition; pairing/CS is **not** independently factorially crossed with those ratios. Per-condition tokenizers are separately trained (80K), and English 50M baselines **repeat** data to 100M; account for tokenizer and repetition. Word/sentence CS improve semantic similarity but little English Zorro/WS; sentence CS has higher PPL, partly switch unpredictability. Low-data semantic gains remain modest. Word similarity picks the best layer on benchmark judgments; not an independent broad reasoning readout.

Nearest ownership constraint: the local library identifies [From Translation to Multilinguality](https://openreview.net/forum?id=1gbJ8euERb) as a direct Standard-vs-Split parent. The primary PDF download currently returns 403/browser verification, including the hashed PDF and API endpoint; full text has **not** been reread in this execution. Treat its ownership conservatively based on the library and indexed primary abstract. Do not claim an unoccupied adjacency/content decomposition; obtain full methods before designing new training.

## Research-scale experiment portfolio

The question is whether bilingual correspondence buys access to a capability, or the capability itself, and under which training state. Do not promote any branch from one pilot.

| Branch | Informative experiment | Evidence required beyond an owned observation | Failure / pivot |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acquisition H1 | JGP pairing contrast; MONOWEB checkpoint trajectories. Independent held-out bits/byte competences, disjoint reasoning items. | Within-family competence × pairing interaction; same tasks; replicate across families. | Weakest languages do not benefit or effect tracks task/content: demote H1. |
| Semantic content H3 | JGP shuffled counterpart vs adjacent; TransWeb translated vs native-register errors. | Separate native knowledge missing from semantic reasoning; control content and dose. | Only generic quality improvement: already owned, no candidate. |
| Cross-language composition | Same semantic items: premise language × choice language; NLI premise × hypothesis language; same-answer-language controls. | Pairing affects compositional binding with minimal output burden, while monolingual task and competence stay stable; task-independent replication. | Effect confined to translated answer likelihood/translation: MONOWEB ownership. |
| Optimization / retention H2 | JGP first/distributed/last; MONOWEB trajectories; Macaroni persistence controls. | Distinguish representation retention, usable readout and task transfer at identical final data. | Generic recency or curriculum advantage: parents own it. |
| Measurement H5 | Full-choice likelihood, length-normalized, unconditional-choice-calibrated; 0/5-shot, paired margins and chance floors. | Show an underlying behavioral capability dissociation on non-floor tasks, not prompt tricks. | Null changes after basic evaluation corrections: pivot away from regime story. |
| Token anchors H6 | False Friends transfer after supervised EN readout vs decoder frozen readout, multilingual lexical burden. | Distinguish shared IDs from shared semantic access with matched content. | Encoder/decoder or task-head difference only: insufficient generality. |

P2 pilot starts with frozen MONOWEB EN-DE and JGP ID/ZH. No new training. Same item IDs and complete scores retained; bootstrap over matched items, not score tables. Early checkpoint trajectories and additional languages follow a valid pilot. P4 remains gated on an identifiable uncertainty existing interventions cannot resolve.

## Scope of the first frozen pilot

- JGP: official XCOPA ID and ZH test items, same IDs/labels/cause-effect checked. Four premise-language × choice-language cells. Natural connector follows premise language. Zero-shot same-language cells match current harness templates; cross-language cells are diagnostics, not published benchmark scores. Retain raw, token-normalized and choice-prior-adjusted likelihood. A prior-adjusted effect disappearing warns that local continuation priors rather than causal reasoning explain the difference.
- MONOWEB: XNLI EN/DE first 500 aligned validation rows, five fixed train examples, four premise × hypothesis cells. Published-harness joint likelihood style; connectors follow premise language. This is an initial reproducibility/measurement pilot, not a full paper benchmark reproduction. Verify floors and connector/label effects before interpreting composition. Sequential row alignment uses the translated XNLI corpus structure plus identical label sequences; semantic spot checks remain necessary.
- No cross-language composition result alone earns novelty: stronger evidence needs invariance to connector/answer format, reduced continuation language burden, same-language competence controls, second task and training family, and targeted ownership audit.
- Data integrity matters: XCOPA ID/ZH has 19 reannotated/incompatible test items; exclude them from all four paired cells (481 common items). `m_hellaswag` preserves semantic item IDs but **permutes ending order across languages**. Join by item ID and use the choice-language gold index; do not assume equal indices. Five HellaSwag demos are excluded from the 500 evaluated items.
- Harness `acc_norm` uses continuation **character** length, not token count. Report `char_norm` as this readout, retain `token_norm` separately as a diagnostic. Explicit short English-label NLI readout is a pre-specified floor check, not a prompt search.
