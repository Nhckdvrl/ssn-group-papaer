## Research-Scale Decision

The original four-parent conflict now has substantial qualifications: OpenSeal does not show broad XCOPA gains; selected JGP exports are not equal-time; the nominal matched comparison did not establish broad reasoning improvement; Macaroni semantic assays are sometimes near floor. These observations weaken the original acquisition-regime story, not the value of investigating bilingual-data causality. Neither another resource axis nor renaming alignment as a circuit will establish novelty.

Stop the near-floor Macaroni toy-task expansion and the unstable joint-error branch. Do not launch generic translation-head discovery, generic shared-representation scoring, or circuit-filtered data training: direct prior covers them. A paper needs an unexpected useful effect and intervention-conditioned mechanism, with acquisition, semantic content coverage and supervision separated.
