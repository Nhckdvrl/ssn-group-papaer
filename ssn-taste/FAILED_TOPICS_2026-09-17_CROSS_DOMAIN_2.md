# Failed Topics — 2026-09-17 Cross-Domain Search II

**Status:** durable kill ledger. Read together with all other `FAILED_TOPICS*` files before generating new topics.

## Search-discipline update

The user does **not** prefer linguistically intricate topics. Do not make fine-grained formal-semantic / psycholinguistic phenomena the default search pool (e.g. coercion, presupposition, indexicals, garden paths, dynamic semantics, obscure syntactic distinctions) unless the mother question is unusually simple, broad, and clearly Main-level. Prefer general mechanisms in learning, generation, representation, training dynamics, multimodality, vision/video, statistics, control, cognitive science, and general ML. Language should often be the experimental medium, not the source of technical obscurity.

These failed topics are **negative/process evidence only**, never positive research-taste exemplars.

## F28 — Covert-event coercion: lexical retrieval versus online enriched composition

**Question.** When an implicit event is recovered from a coercive expression, is it retrieved from lexical/event memory or constructed online compositionally?

**Nearest prior.** Classic psycholinguistics already contrasts retrieval with enriched composition; recent CoNLL/LM work directly studies whether neural models recover covert events compositionally and finds strong lexical/distributional effects.

**Kill reason.** The scientific distinction is already mature and modern LM work has transported it into current models. A hidden-state/causal follow-up would be reviewer-compressible to mechanism analysis of an existing coercion parent. It is also too linguistically intricate for the preferred search taste.

**Revival condition.** Only if a much broader non-linguistic computational distinction emerges that coercion is merely one clean test case for.

## F45 — Semantic retraction / mid-utterance repair in append-only Transformers

**Question.** In `Turn left—sorry, right`, how can a causal Transformer make the reparandum cease to determine current meaning when old K/V states cannot be deleted? Filter, overlay/coexistence, rebinding, or readout gating?

**Nearest prior.** EMNLP 2023 Findings already studies LLM mid-utterance repair/changing-one’s-mind behavior and incremental left-to-right limitations; EMNLP 2024 Main and related work study belief revision/suppression after new evidence.

**Kill reason.** Reviewer-level story compresses to “known repair/revision behavior → now analyze mechanism,” exactly the mature-anomaly follow-up pattern to avoid. Also increasingly language-specific.

**What to learn.** A potentially powerful future generator is **one piece of information losing one computational role while retaining another**; seek a non-language-specific object with this dual-status structure.

**Revival condition.** A different operation where information loses one role but retains another, without an existing behavioral parent.

## F51 — Training objective A versus independent quantity B: common ground / commitment / coherence / plausibility

**Question family.** Next-token likelihood optimizes corpus probability, whereas language use may require independent quantities such as common ground, speaker commitment, discourse coherence, or semantic plausibility. Do these quantities emerge as separable internal variables?

**Nearest prior.** Each candidate quantity already has a mature NLP literature; common ground reaches ACL 2026 Main, speaker commitment has long-standing datasets/work, and coherence/plausibility have extensive LM literatures.

**Kill reason.** The successful `likelihood ≠ grammaticality` framing cannot be mechanically cloned by substituting another already-mature quantity B.

**What to learn.** An A≠B topic is promising only when B is theoretically independent *and* has not already become its own NLP research lane.

**Revival condition.** A genuinely under-owned quantity B with a clear independent scientific definition and opposing predictions to A.
