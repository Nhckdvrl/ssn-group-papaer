# Topic decision criteria, recorded while models load

E001a supplies a contextual interpretation diagnostic, not the original decisive
pilot. Its role in the topic decision is deliberately limited.

1. Report all 42 examples, both models, both contexts, both label orders; no tuning
   or new exclusions after outputs. Record complete-pair accuracy as well as item
   accuracy, and disclose whether a model's highest-scoring token is outside A/B.
2. A full-context advantage establishes a signal worth studying on this fixed set.
   No advantage does not refute discourse use: single-sentence evidence, source
   noise, long-context processing and familiar-story pretraining all remain possible.
3. Neither high nor low interpretation accuracy establishes H01/H02/H03. In
   particular, prediction DNI on an INI is not a measured generated hallucination.
4. Mainline approval requires a new consequence beyond DUST/RefNLI/Roit/REGen,
   an independently defensible support target, and evidence appropriate to that
   target. Model scale and source provenance cannot substitute for target validity.
5. Distinguish rejection of the **current operational route** from a proof that no
   future paper is possible. If all inspected resources miss the target and the
   executed study only supports an occupied classification narrative, state a
   no-go on advancing this package as a NAACL Main mainline. Document the
   substantive redesign needed to reopen it; do not manufacture an easy claim.
