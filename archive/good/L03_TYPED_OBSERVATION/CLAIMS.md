# L03 claim and evidence ledger

**Updated:** 2026-09-08. **Paper mainline:** not approved.

Every substantive claim needs an ID, scope, independent evidence or experiment,
status, and a disconfirmation criterion. Planned claims are not findings. Software
tests are not model experiments. Existing C1/C2/C3 text describes objectives only.

| ID | Proposition and scope | Evidence / required experiment | Status | Limit or disconfirmation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D01 | ACS officially distinguishes certain nonnumeric representations from ordinary estimates. | Official notes read through web retrieval; mapping in experiment README. | D03 | Annotated interval medians are not exact scalar medians. | Official median interval definitions; E000 conservative parser. | Provider-documented; occurrence unmeasured | A bound is retained separately; actual dataset coverage remains unverified. |

| C1 | Explicit typing changes typed-observation correctness relative to direct generation. | Future E001 paired comparisons with matched evidence and appropriate computation controls. | HYPOTHESIS | A score improvement alone cannot establish general necessity or identify its cause. |
| C2 | Provider/status characteristics predict where typing matters. | Prespecified subgroup interactions and held-out provider evaluation. | HYPOTHESIS | ACS-only or post-hoc subsets cannot establish transfer or a reliable boundary. |
| C3 | A scalar-centric evaluation changes a scientific/modeling conclusion. | Score the same predictions using validated conventional and typed criteria; specify decision thresholds in advance. | HYPOTHESIS | A different score scale alone is insufficient. |
| P01 | Direct generation is equivalent or non-inferior in specified regimes. | Prespecified margins, power, paired uncertainty, and replication. | ALTERNATIVE HYPOTHESIS | Failure to reject a difference is not preservation evidence. |
