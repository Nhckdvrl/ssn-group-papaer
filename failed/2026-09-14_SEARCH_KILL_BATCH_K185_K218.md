## Batch-level lessons

1. **Do not resurrect by domain substitution.** Most parents above were killed at the level of scientific quantity, not dataset.
2. **Do not manufacture contradictions between different estimands.** K207 is the canonical warning: raw task gradient, Fisher information, plasticity, causal importance, and editability are not interchangeable.
3. **Recent award papers generate successor programs extremely quickly.** A clean `future work` sentence is weak evidence of open parent ownership.
4. **A cleaner causal control is not automatically a new paper.** K210 is the canonical reviewer-compression case.
5. **Changed-regime revisits need a changed load-bearing premise, not merely a newer model.** K185–K218 contain multiple examples where this condition failed.
